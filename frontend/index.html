About EyePredict

EyePredict is powered by MobileNetV2, an advanced deep convolutional neural network optimized for lightweight and high-accuracy image classification. It efficiently extracts retinal features through depthwise separable convolutions, capturing fine vessel patterns, optic disc details, and lesion regions. Grad-CAM visualization highlights the exact retinal areas influencing the model’s decision, providing clear interpretability and supporting trustworthy AI-assisted diagnosis.

🔬 Technology

Trained CNN model (MobileNet-like backbone) processed images at 224×224; Grad-CAM is computed on the last conv layer.

⚙️ Functionality

Upload → Model predicts disease and confidence → Grad-CAM image returned and shown inline for quick inspection.

⚠️ Disclaimer

AI assistant only — not a replacement for clinical diagnosis. Always consult an eye care professional for medical decisions.
